HEARTH · Phase 1 · reviewer backup

Care information, made executable.

This stable page remains available if the interactive reviewer path is interrupted. HEARTH is a TRL-3 browser proof of concept using an entirely synthetic household.

Open the interactive HEARTH prototype

Safety boundary: no clinical decision, message, appointment, purchase, or record change occurs. Provider and resource responses are labeled simulations.

Three-to-five-minute path

  1. Open the prototype and choose Reset Reviewer Demo.
  2. Choose Reviewer demo.
  3. Correct the uncertain extraction and prepare the pharmacist question.
  4. Record the simulated professional resolution.
  5. Assign transportation using minimum-necessary disclosure.
  6. Verify equipment and qualified wound-care support.
  7. Redistribute caregiver workload and record provider acknowledgement.
  8. Escalate the unknown instruction.
  9. Inspect accountability receipts, evidence, and remaining controls.

Evidence snapshot

  • Smart 40: 40/40 controlled cases.
  • Focused benchmark: 60/60 controlled cases.
  • External-style holdout: 15/20 on the first locked run; five failures retained.
  • Burden-study instrument: implemented; 0 participants and no measured time-saving claim.
  • Real caregiver interview records: not supplied; no participant evidence is claimed.
  • Partners: candidates identified; no commitment claimed.
All people, records, and care events shown by HEARTH are synthetic. Not for clinical use.
