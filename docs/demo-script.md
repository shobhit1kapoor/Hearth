# Guided demo script

Target duration: 3–5 minutes. Start in **Guided reviewer demo** and select **Reset demo**.

## 0:00–0:35 · Scope

Open **Welcome & scope**. State:

- HEARTH is execution assurance, not a chatbot or symptom checker.
- The case is synthetic and the initial mission is `NOT EXECUTABLE`.
- External connections are controlled simulations.

## 0:35–1:10 · Inputs and compiled responsibilities

Open **Care inbox** and show ten dated sources, review states, supersession, and responsibility counts. Open **Compilation review** and expand one CCO to show owner, source excerpt, confidence, safety level, permission, completion evidence, backup, escalation, and history.

Record the uncertain voice-note correction. Emphasize that the original remains.

## 1:10–1:45 · Reality Check

Open **Reality check**. Show:

- conflicting insulin instructions;
- no qualified wound-care owner;
- unavailable scale;
- caregiver capacity deficit;
- unaccepted transportation;
- unclear instruction and unacknowledged provider message.

Point out that unaffected responsibilities may continue.

## 1:45–2:25 · Medication and minimum disclosure

Open **Medication safety**. Show both exact source versions. Prepare the pharmacist question, then record the controlled professional response and Maya’s confirmation.

Open **Appointments**. Preview Daniel’s transportation request. Show diagnosis, medication, insurance, and private notes withheld. Record acceptance.

## 2:25–3:25 · Execution repair

Return to **Guided reviewer demo** and run:

- scale verification;
- qualified wound-care support;
- evening workload redistribution;
- provider acknowledgement;
- abstention and professional escalation for the unknown instruction.

Show the mission become `READY WITH CONTROLS`, not unconditional Ready.

## 3:25–4:15 · Audit and evidence

Open **Accountability receipts** to show information used, shared, withheld, permission, approval, outcome, uncertainty, and next step.

Open **Evidence & validation**:

- Smart 40: 40/40 consecutive controlled cases;
- focused benchmark: 60/60;
- Protocol 9-Delta exact abstention;
- time saved and interview evidence honestly unmeasured/unavailable;
- simulations and limitations clearly labeled.

## Reviewer close

HEARTH’s claim is narrow: under controlled synthetic conditions, fragmented care information can become a permission-aware, capacity-aware, closed-loop mission that stops safely when it does not know.
