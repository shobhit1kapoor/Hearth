# Accessibility known limitations

- No human screen-reader session with a caregiver has been completed.
- The 200% zoom check remains a required manual verification on the public deployment.
- Touch targets and virtual-keyboard behavior have not been tested across a representative physical-device matrix.
- Axe cannot evaluate cognitive load, plain-language comprehension, voice-control usability, or whether the timing instrument creates fatigue.
- The final DOCX files pass the repository's structural checker and the PDFs are tagged, but no independent Section 508 conformance review has occurred.
- The timed study contains a timer. Participants may pause or stop, but a future study review should verify that time pressure does not disadvantage participants with disabilities.

These limitations block any claim of independent certification or universal accessibility.
