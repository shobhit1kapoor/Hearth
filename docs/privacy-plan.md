# Privacy plan

Privacy is modeled as executable product state rather than a footer.

## Implemented Phase 1 controls

- Care-recipient permission choices are a dated source.
- Permissions are purpose-specific, role-specific, time-limited, and revocable.
- Transportation access includes logistics and mobility support while withholding diagnosis, medication, insurance, and caregiver-private notes.
- Professional review receives only the sources relevant to the assigned responsibility.
- Caregiver-private notes are a separate category.
- Prepared disclosures state what is shared and intentionally withheld.
- Access, permission changes, denials, corrections, and external preparations appear in the synthetic audit log.
- Export and deletion-request controls are visible and honestly simulated.
- All demonstration records are synthetic.
- No health-data advertising, sale, or training use is claimed or designed.

## Production requirements

- Data inventory and purpose limitation for every field.
- Household-scoped access policy enforced in the service layer and database.
- Encrypted transport and storage with managed keys.
- Retention schedule, legal hold, backup expiration, deletion verification, and export authentication.
- Vendor agreements, model-retention configuration, privacy review, and incident-response obligations.
- Consent accessibility and supported decision-making evaluation with care recipients.

HEARTH is designed with privacy and security controls appropriate for controlled Phase 1 validation, with specific compliance obligations to be determined with Phase 2 deployment partners. No HIPAA certification or compliance attestation is claimed.
