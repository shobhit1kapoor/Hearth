# Burden-study consent template

This is an unsigned planning template. It is not evidence that consent was obtained and must be reviewed by the responsible research lead before use.

**Study purpose.** We are evaluating how people organize a synthetic post-discharge care scenario using a manual workflow and a controlled HEARTH prototype.

**What participation involves.** You will complete the same eight coordination tasks in two conditions. The session records task time, interactions, help requests, corrections, completion, confidence, effort, and optional feedback. Expected duration: 45-60 minutes including orientation and breaks.

**Not medical care.** All case information is synthetic. HEARTH is a Phase 1 proof of concept, not medical advice, a clinical system, or an emergency service.

**Voluntary participation.** Participation is optional. You may skip any question, take a break, or stop without penalty.

**Privacy.** The study uses an anonymous participant code. Do not provide names, diagnoses, real care details, phone numbers, email addresses, or other identifying information. Any approved code-to-contact linkage is stored separately by the research team.

**Risks and benefits.** Possible risks include fatigue, frustration, or discomfort discussing coordination work. There is no guaranteed personal benefit. Findings may inform a safer and less burdensome Phase 2 design.
