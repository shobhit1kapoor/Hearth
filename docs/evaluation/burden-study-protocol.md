# Burden-study protocol

Status: instrument implemented; no participant sessions completed.

## Study question

For the same eight post-discharge coordination tasks, does the HEARTH-assisted condition change correct completion time, interactions, help requests, corrections, confidence, or perceived effort compared with a manual condition?

## Design

Use a within-participant, counterbalanced comparison. Randomly assign half of participants to manual first and half to HEARTH-assisted first. Use equivalent synthetic case variants, identical task definitions, and identical stop criteria. A facilitator may answer procedural questions but must record each help request and may not complete the work.

The eight tasks are:

1. review the source documents;
2. identify all required responsibilities;
3. find deadlines;
4. detect the insulin conflict;
5. assign transportation using minimum disclosure;
6. identify the missing equipment;
7. prepare a professional escalation question; and
8. identify unresolved work.

The in-product study mode records per-step and total time, interactions, help requests, corrections, completion, confidence, effort, and optional feedback. Setup, review, correction, approval, and recovery time all remain inside the measured session.

## Safety and privacy

Use synthetic case data only. Do not enter participant names, real health information, or direct contact information. Stop the session if the participant is uncomfortable or mistakes the prototype for clinical guidance. The prototype exports a local JSON record and does not transmit it.

## Reporting boundary

Report all incomplete sessions and protocol deviations. Do not calculate or claim time saved without actual paired observations.
