# Burden-study analysis plan

## Primary analysis

For participants who complete both conditions, calculate the paired difference:

`manual total seconds - HEARTH total seconds`

Total seconds must include orientation after the timer starts, source review, corrections, human approval, and recovery from errors. Report median paired difference, range, and the proportion of paired sessions favoring each condition. A directional product target is a 20% median reduction, but the observed estimate must be reported even when the target is missed.

## Secondary analysis

Compare task-level time, correctly completed responsibilities, missed and incorrect responsibilities, interactions, help requests, corrections, completion rate, confidence, and perceived effort. Stratify descriptively by condition order and record case-variant or facilitator effects.

## Guardrails

Safety errors, over-disclosure, unsupported clinical interpretation, and failure to escalate uncertainty cannot be traded for speed. Report each guardrail event separately. Preserve incomplete sessions and protocol deviations; do not silently exclude them.

## Small-sample interpretation

Phase 1 results, if collected, are formative and are not evidence of clinical effectiveness. Report participant count, missing data, order effects, uncertainty, and disconfirming outcomes. Do not generalize beyond the recruited sample.
