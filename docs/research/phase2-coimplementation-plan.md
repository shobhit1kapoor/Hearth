# Phase 2 co-implementation plan

## Governance

- Paid caregiver and care-recipient council with decision rights, not advisory optics.
- Representation across dementia, disability, caregiver relationships, rural and low-bandwidth contexts, language, race/ethnicity, income, and digital confidence.
- Accessible materials, flexible participation, support people, and multiple feedback channels.

## Cadence

- Months 1–2: consent, language, safety, onboarding, and evaluation co-design.
- Months 3–4: weekly controlled-pilot review and rapid correction.
- Months 5–9: monthly implementation council plus targeted subgroup sessions.
- Months 10–12: interpretation of results, safety-case revision, and Phase 3 decisions.

## Decision scope

Participants will influence what data is collected, how access is explained, which actions may be prepared, what counts as burden reduction, escalation language, notification cadence, accessibility, and whether a feature should be removed.

The plan requires documented partners, consent, compensation, and evaluation review before recruitment. None is currently claimed.
