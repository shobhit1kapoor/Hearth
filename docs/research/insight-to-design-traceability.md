# Insight-to-design traceability

> Current evidence source: verified webinar themes in the user-supplied brief, not HEARTH caregiver interviews.

| Context theme | Design response | Working evidence | Research status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invisible coordination work | Care Commitment Objects and full-mission feasibility | Compilation Review and Reality Check | Must validate with interviews |
| Caregivers do not want another homework assignment | One safe next action and grouped updates | Today’s Mission | Must test cognitive load |
| Different automation preferences | Organize, Recommend, Prepare, Delegate | Permission Vault | Must test preference differences |
| Privacy and trust concerns | Minimum-necessary, expiring, revocable access | Permission Vault and appointments | Must test comprehension |
| AI should not replace relationships | Administrative preparation and closed-loop tracking | Product scope | Human-connection outcome unmeasured |
| Real-world evidence matters | Transparent synthetic metrics and Phase 2 protocol | Evidence screen | Independent evaluation pending |
