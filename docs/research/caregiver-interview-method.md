# Caregiver interview method

> Missing-evidence warning: no interview transcripts, recordings, summaries, survey answers, or notes were available in this workspace. No interviews are claimed.

When materials and consent are available:

1. Assign anonymized participant identifiers.
2. Remove direct identifiers and unnecessary health information.
3. Preserve context needed to interpret caregiving work.
4. Use two reviewers where feasible for coding and disagreement resolution.
5. Separate direct quotation, participant interpretation, researcher interpretation, and product implication.
6. Report frequency cautiously and never imply representativeness from a small sample.
7. Include care recipients and disabled people directly where appropriate.
8. Compensate participants and make accessible participation available.

The interview guide should probe discharge coordination, invisible work, source conflict, permissions, trust, correction, automation preferences, burden added by tools, and conditions that require human help.
