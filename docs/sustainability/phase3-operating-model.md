# Phase 3 operating model

## Directional model

The current six-month, 100-household pilot model totals $145,000, or $1,450 per enrolled household. At an assumed 75 average monthly active households, that is approximately $1,933 per monthly active household for the pilot period. This is a high-touch evaluation model, not a mature software price.

Directional annual operating scenarios:

| Scale | Assumed annual operating cost | Cost per active household-year |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1,000 active households | $380,000 | $380 |
| 10,000 active households | $1,700,000 | $170 |

The estimates include model processing, storage, human review, support, accessibility support, organization onboarding, security/compliance operations, and integration work. They exclude clinical care, devices, and any reimbursement not yet established.

## Operating responsibilities

- A product and safety team owns scope, incident response, change control, and release gates.
- Caregiver advisers and accessibility reviewers participate as compensated governance roles.
- Qualified professionals define escalation boundaries; HEARTH does not substitute for them.
- Partner onboarding includes data-flow review, permission mapping, support ownership, downtime procedures, and exit/export procedures.
- Independent evaluators review burden, safety, equity, and disconfirming evidence.

## Cost reduction without reducing safeguards

Use smaller fit-for-purpose models, deterministic preprocessing, caching of non-sensitive static content, short retention by default, organization-supported onboarding, shared accessibility assets, and narrow integration adapters. Do not reduce cost by removing human approval, professional escalation, export/deletion controls, accessibility support, or participant compensation.

These figures are planning assumptions, not a reimbursement, procurement, revenue, or sustainability commitment.
