# Phase 3 scalability and sustainability

## Implementation pathway

- Deploy with multiple transition-of-care, aging/disability, caregiver-support, and community-service organizations.
- Expand geographic, racial, socioeconomic, language, disability, caregiver-relationship, and connectivity diversity.
- Add caregiving conditions only after condition-specific workflow and safety review.
- Implement standards-based health information exchange where partners authorize it.
- Build community-service directories and closed-loop referral outcomes.
- Commission independent effectiveness, safety, accessibility, fairness, and implementation evaluation.
- Publish an implementation playbook, safety-case template, training materials, and support model.

## Operating model

- Multi-stakeholder governance with paid caregiver and care-recipient voting representation.
- Central product, security, model-risk, accessibility, and support functions.
- Local configuration owned jointly with each deployment organization.
- Versioned models and adapters with monitoring, rollback, and transparent changes.
- Help channels that do not require high digital literacy.

## Affordability

- Smartphone and web access with printable output.
- Future text and email pathways and low-bandwidth mode.
- No required proprietary hardware.
- No advertising based on health information and no sale of personal data.
- Free controlled pilot access.
- Evaluate organization-sponsored and low-cost essential caregiver access.
- Measure total cost per supported household, including setup, support, integration, and human review.

No exact price, reimbursement, guaranteed federal adoption, or partner financing is asserted.
