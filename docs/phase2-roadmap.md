# Phase 2 roadmap

## Months 1–2 · safety and co-implementation

- Form a paid caregiver and care-recipient council with representation across dementia, disability, rural, low-bandwidth, language, and accessibility needs.
- Secure transition-of-care, aging/disability, evaluation, privacy, security, and clinical-safety partners.
- Finalize consent, supported decision-making, retention, escalation, adverse-event, and incident protocols.
- Conduct independent threat, accessibility, and clinical workflow review.
- Register the evaluation plan and lock initial outcome definitions.

## Months 3–4 · controlled pilot

- Approximately 15 caregiver–care recipient pairs, subject to final protocol and partner capacity.
- Guided onboarding and frequent safety review.
- Import selected real documents with participant permission.
- No autonomous clinical decisions or unsupervised high-risk actions.
- Weekly error, correction, burden, permission, and accessibility review.

## Months 5–9 · expanded implementation

- Add participants and settings based on early safety evidence.
- Test administrative workflows, human escalation, adapter nonresponse, low bandwidth, print, and accessibility.
- Evaluate performance across caregiver relationship, disability, dementia, language, geography, and digital-access subgroups where sample sizes permit responsible interpretation.
- Introduce limited, approved administrative integrations behind idempotent adapters.

## Months 10–12 · evaluation and readiness

- Analyze workflow accuracy, burden, safety, subgroup performance, correction success, cost, and implementation fit.
- Update safety case, threat model, consent, model governance, and product.
- Publish known failures and independent evaluator findings.
- Produce the Phase 3 implementation, support, governance, and financing plan.

This roadmap does not claim that the needed partners or participants are already committed.
