# Independent evaluation review request

Subject: Request for independent review of the HEARTH Phase 2 evaluation plan

We request a methodological review of the locked holdout process and proposed within-participant burden study. The review should examine:

- ground-truth creation and separation from implementation;
- counterbalancing, order effects, facilitator effects, and missing data;
- correct measurement of setup, review, approval, correction, and recovery time;
- safety and privacy guardrails that cannot be traded for speed;
- treatment of retained failures and disconfirming results; and
- the limits of inference from a small formative sample.

Requested commitment: one protocol review, a short written risk memo, and optional verification of the preregistered Phase 2 analysis before data collection. The reviewer would not be asked to certify effectiveness or endorse HEARTH.
