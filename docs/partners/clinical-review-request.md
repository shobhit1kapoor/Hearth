# Clinical-boundary review request

Subject: Request for bounded clinical and transition-workflow review of HEARTH

HEARTH is a controlled Phase 1 prototype focused on family coordination during the first 30 days after an adult with dementia and complex conditions returns home. It does not recommend treatment or select medication instructions.

We are requesting a documented review of:

- the boundary between logistical coordination and professional clinical judgment;
- medication-conflict, shorthand, unknown-instruction, and escalation behavior;
- evidence required before a responsibility can be marked complete;
- safe handling of provider non-response and adapter outage; and
- the clinical plausibility of the proposed Phase 2 evaluation guardrails.

Requested commitment: one 60-minute review, written comments on the boundary matrix, and optional participation in two Phase 2 advisory sessions. This is not a request for endorsement, production use, or uncompensated clinical responsibility.
