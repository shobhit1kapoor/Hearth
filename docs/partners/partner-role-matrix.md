# Partner role matrix

Status: candidate roles only; no outreach or commitments completed.

| Partner type | Requested contribution | Phase 2 evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caregiver-support organization | Recruit and support compensated caregiver advisers | Interview records, consent status, burden-study sessions, disconfirming feedback | No implied endorsement |
| Dementia or aging organization | Review dignity, choice, inclusion, and workflow fit | Advisory notes and design-change traceability | No clinical responsibility |
| Pharmacist | Review medication-conflict and escalation boundaries | Signed or attributable boundary review | Does not select care for study cases |
| Nurse or transition-of-care professional | Review handoff realism and completion evidence | Workflow and failure-mode review | No production deployment claim |
| Accessibility expert | Review keyboard, screen-reader, cognitive, and low-vision needs | Independent findings and remediation log | No certification unless explicitly contracted |
| Evaluation adviser | Review holdout, burden study, and analysis | Protocol memo and preregistration review | Independence and conflicts disclosed |
| Health or community organization | Integration discovery and pilot feasibility | Data-flow questions, operating assumptions, scoped letter if agreed | No integration or procurement claim |

Candidate organizations remain in `outreach-list.md`; engagement remains zero until a dated log entry and source evidence are added.
