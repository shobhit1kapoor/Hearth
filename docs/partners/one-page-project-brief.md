# HEARTH partner brief

## The gap

Healthcare gives families instructions, but no system owns the space between those instructions and real life. In the first 30 days after an adult with dementia and complex conditions returns home, responsibilities are scattered across discharge pages, portal messages, pharmacy calls, family availability, transportation, equipment, and changing professional guidance.

## The Phase 1 concept

HEARTH converts fragmented synthetic care information into source-grounded commitments that are authorized, safely assigned, completed, and verified while protecting caregiver capacity and care-recipient choice. The controlled prototype refuses unknown protocols, preserves medication conflicts, shares minimum-necessary logistics, requires outcome evidence before completion, and keeps professional decisions with qualified professionals.

## Evidence boundary

HEARTH is a TRL-3 proof of concept using synthetic data and simulated adapters. It is not medical advice, a production clinical system, a HIPAA certification claim, or evidence of caregiver outcomes. The controlled suites pass 40/40 and 60/60; the first locked external-style holdout passes 15/20 with five failures retained, and the separate post-holdout remediation suite passes 5/5.
